Privacy Policy

Motivator: Daily Quotes · Last updated: November 2026 · Support · Русский · Español

We do not collect your personal data on our servers, we do not know who you are, and there is no account to log in to. Below is what actually happens on your device and in the services we rely on, and what you control.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account, email address, phone number or any other sign-up.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites. The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is no tracking to agree to.

What stays on your device

  • Your name (optional, from the first questions) is used only to personalize the app and reminder text. We never receive it.
  • Your answers (gender, age range, goals, concerns, preferred styles) and your selected categories are stored on the device so the feed can be tailored.
  • Your favorites, your own quotes, your photo and your reminder schedule are kept on your device, and we cannot see them.
  • Widgets read the same local storage, so they work without a network.
  • Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device. The copy in your iCloud (see below) stays, and comes back when you install the app again. To erase both at once, use Profile, Delete my data.

What leaves your device

  • Your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your profile (name, answers, settings), your photo, favorites and own quotes in your private iCloud storage (CloudKit), so that a new install or a new phone starts where the last one ended. That storage is yours: only you can read it, it counts toward your own iCloud space, and we have no access to it. You can remove it in Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.
  • Purchases. When you start a trial or subscribe, Apple processes the purchase. Apple gives the app a receipt that is used only to know whether you have Premium. We do not see your Apple ID, your card or your name.
  • Reminders. They are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing goes through our servers.
  • No analytics. The app does not send usage statistics or any identifier to us or to third parties.
  • Diagnostics. If the app crashes and Share Analytics is turned on in your device settings, Apple may share crash reports with us. They are anonymous and used only to fix bugs.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from children. There is nothing to opt into because nothing personal is collected.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects what leaves your device, we will update the date above and mention it in the release notes.
