A quote for every day, on your Home Screen and in gentle reminders. If something does not work or you have an idea, write to karengaloian@yandex.ru. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually answer within a few days.

How do I get Premium back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the purchase screen and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to the device.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Motivator: Daily Quotes. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap “+” in the top-left corner, find Motivator: Daily Quotes and pick a size. The small and medium widgets are free; the large and Lock Screen widgets are part of Premium.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings, Notifications, Motivator: Daily Quotes, and that reminders are on in the app’s Profile. Reminders are planned a week ahead, so open the app at least once a week to keep them coming.

My favorites did not come back after reinstalling

Your profile, favorites, own quotes and photo are kept in your private iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID as before, then open the app and wait a moment while it syncs.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete my data. This removes your profile, photo, favorites and own quotes from the device and from your iCloud. Your subscription is not affected.
